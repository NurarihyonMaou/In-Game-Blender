===== How to Toggle Zibai ======

     [F6] ON and OFF all Mods
   [F10] Save the Mod

       [/] ON and OFF Help Menu (1st page)
[ALT /] ON and OFF Help Menu (2nd page)

[ALT .] Lock Toggles

------------------------------------------------

Use [CTRL] with other keys
to toggle in reverse order

       [P] Garterbelt

       [5] Stockings
[ALT 5] Eyes & Makeup

       [6] Hair
[ALT 6] Ears & Neck

       [Y] Dress & Corset
[ALT Y] Bra

       [H] Panties
[ALT H] Back Tattoo

       [N] Shoes & Feet
[ALT N] Tail

       [7] Hands & Gloves
[ALT 7] Coat & Armlets

       [8] Body Piercing
[ALT 8] Earrings & Glasses
