----------------------- Complex Combinations -----------------------

You can combine first type of Nails for bare Feet [N]
to make finger Stockings [5]

Combine Original Neck option [ALT 6] with any Coat [ALT 7]
and any SFW top of the Dress [Y] for the Transparent Piece
above the breasts to appear

Combine Garterbelt [P] with original Panties [H]
or with the Corset [Y] for unique results

 
-------------------------- Worth Mentioning --------------------------

You can change Keys used in the Toggles
at the top of the Zibai.ini file


----------------------------------- Fixes -----------------------------------

Change characters in your party a few times
or restart the game to fix missing or flickering body parts

Change the name of the mod without using special characters
to fix the mod not Saving your combination [F10]

Download/Update ORFix to fix broken textures, shadows and outlines

Download/Update TexFx to fix transparency for Glasses [ALT 8]
